# FrayID public roadmap

Status: R01 registered; local source/data binding passed; prebuilt runtime artifact missing; no active execution
V1 geometry: complete and frozen

> **Current decision (2026-09-03):** the primary path is official/reference
> SelfRecon-style reproduction, then an isolated project-video adapter, then a
> unified outer-surface fit. Earlier text that calls L03 CUDA or a MANTLE stage
> the next action is historical and superseded. R01 now requires one separately
> authorized private build of its frozen content-addressed runtime recipe. No
> capture, CUDA run, paid job, or scientific attempt is currently authorized.

This roadmap describes possible next research without reopening the accepted
private test or expanding silently into product features.

## Principles

- Preserve the accepted V1 candidate, thresholds, and one-time sealed test.
- Evaluate one mechanism at a time with a predeclared stop condition.
- Require synthetic/local gates before paid cloud execution.
- Keep topology validity active during optimization, not only at export.
- Separate development validation, final test, and qualitative review.
- Keep all human media and derived geometry local/private.
- Publish methods and synthetic fixtures where possible, not private subject
  artifacts.

## Current research boundary

There is no active native scientific experiment. E25, B2, and B3 are closed.
P0 resolved the
shared replay defect; therefore replay remains a
mandatory gate rather than an open workaround. The
strongest unresolved fact is that the
accepted carrier passes the project's original-reference orientation/area and
ordinary manifold tests but is not an exactly embedded closed surface. That
does not authorize post-hoc repair: a repair changes canonical geometry and
would require a new hypothesis, common-source control, topology/fidelity gates,
and proof that improvements do not come from deleting or hiding difficult
surface regions.

E10 narrows this boundary further: Alpha Wrapping at the publicly frozen scale
removed self-intersections but retained three handles. Another parameter was
not tried because doing so after the real result would violate the frozen
selection rule. A future source constructor must therefore address genus by
construction or by a separately registered, fidelity-accounted mechanism—not
by silent hole filling or candidate shopping.

E11-E15 and P2/P3 narrow it again. An exact Euler-2 start and exact dyadic
refinement are now available as reusable public primitives, but global pressure
locks and full or statically partitioned Tight-Inclusion remains too expensive
on the refined near-contact hairpin. The next proposal must reduce dense
near-field work itself—such as through independently certified local feasible
regions—rather than merely cull far pairs. It needs a new preregistration and
cannot reduce the hairpin resolution or weaken exact endpoint checks.

Other open questions remain independent. E4 shows that certified feasible
directions alone did not clear the mature real smoke. E5 shows that clean
material identity can improve geometry and motion, but the tested robust loss
and replay contract were insufficient. A new proposal should state which one
of source embeddedness, motion/evidence attribution, or field conversion it
tests, and should not combine closed treatments unless interaction itself is
the registered hypothesis.

The highest-information-gain unregistered hypothesis is a new-connectivity,
exactly embedded carrier built from an inherently embedded implicit or
volumetric extraction. Its first gate would be source-only: exact zero self-
intersections, watertight oriented Euler-2 topology, deterministic skinning
transfer, exact replay, and fidelity to immutable V1 distance, normals, and
volume before any image optimization. It is not an E6/E7 repair and is not
authorized to run. Opaque visibility and track robustness remain separate
later questions.

## R5 — Reproducibility and maintainability

Priority: high. This work improves confidence without changing private V1
geometry.

Possible deliverables:

- package the synthetic rotating clothed-ellipsoid sequence as a fully public
  fixture;
- add a deterministic CPU mini-pipeline that exercises frame selection,
  evidence validation, initialization contracts, optimization, extraction, and
  evaluation without proprietary weights;
- publish machine-readable metric schemas and topology reports with synthetic
  examples;
- add public export CI that rebuilds the mirror, audits forbidden content, and
  compares the generated tree with the public branch;
- document coordinate frames and tensor shapes at every module boundary;
- add profiling for rendering, closest-distance queries, and topology
  projection.

Gate: public CI reproduces synthetic metrics within fixed tolerances on a clean
machine and the public repository remains free of private identifiers.

## R6 — Multi-sequence robustness

Priority: high if new consented data becomes available. One private sequence
cannot establish generalization.

Design a new study with multiple subjects, clothing shapes, motions, lighting
conditions, and capture devices. Define consent, retention, deletion, access,
and failure-reporting rules before collection. Each sequence needs its own
train/validation/test integrity, while population-level summaries must not
expose individual geometry.

Candidate measurements:

- initialization pass rate;
- evidence completeness and rejection reasons;
- held-out IoU, boundary, and normal distributions;
- topology failure rate and backtracking pressure;
- sensitivity to frame count, angular coverage, motion, and clothing topology;
- calibration and pose failure categories.

Gate: thresholds and analysis are fixed before aggregate results are opened.
V1's private sealed test is never reused for selection.

## R7 — Better sequence motion and calibration

Priority: medium. The experiment history suggests pose/camera/deformation
coupling is more important than adding unrestricted geometry capacity.

Candidate mechanisms:

- spline or Lie-group temporal priors for root and joint motion;
- confidence-aware joint and silhouette fusion with explicit outlier states;
- shared intrinsics plus bounded low-frequency calibration drift when supported
  by independent evidence;
- alternating motion/geometry blocks with trust regions and optimizer-moment
  damping;
- uncertainty estimates for transforms so unreliable frames contribute less
  without using held-out outcomes for selection.

The previously tested cubic trajectory and material-track formulation are
closed and must not be rerun by changing knots, track thresholds, or budget. A
different temporal or correspondence mechanism requires a new falsifiable
hypothesis and synthetic control.

Gate: improve development geometry without widening train/held-out gap or
increasing topology pressure. Stop if gains come only from a few manually
selected frames.

## R8 — Direct field optimization

Priority: medium/experimental. V1 uses a stable explicit carrier and then a
topology-safe SDF bridge. A future system may optimize the field more directly,
but prior neural, dense-grid, tetrahedral, and interface-conforming attempts
failed for structural reasons.

Candidate options:

- a multiresolution dense SDF with fixed-sign topology constraints;
- a neural SDF supervised by exact closest-distance samples and explicit
  surface normals, with topology evaluated throughout training;
- a tetrahedral/hybrid field initialized with conservative occupancy and an
  adaptive minimum-area floor;
- differentiable topology-aware projection between implicit and explicit
  representations.

The certified convex solver from E4 may remain as shared numerical
infrastructure, but E4 training cannot be retried or extended under its old ID.
Likewise, E6's accepted-source collision failure cannot be fixed by deleting
intersections or returning the input surface from the field extractor. Any
source-embeddedness change is a new geometry experiment with an unchanged
comparator and explicit fidelity accounting.

Required preflight:

- sphere and rotated thin-bridge fixtures;
- finite Eikonal and renderer gradients;
- sub-voxel topology preservation;
- deterministic extraction;
- bidirectional Chamfer and normal error no worse than the accepted bridge;
- no extra connected components, flips, collapses, or boundary clipping.

Do not promote an option merely because its training loss falls.

## R9 — Rendering modernization

Priority: low unless current sampling becomes the measured bottleneck.

The present reference-density point renderer passed convergence tests. An exact
triangle renderer should be revisited only with a formal equivalence suite for
projection, visibility, antialiasing, silhouette, boundary, normals, and
gradient direction. The earlier triangle implementation passed synthetic
gradients but failed the real metric-preservation contract.

Gate: existing accepted checkpoints must retain metrics within predeclared
tolerance before any new training uses the renderer.

## R10 — Appearance, explicitly deferred

Priority: deferred. RGB appearance is a separate research aim and must not be
mixed into V1 geometry acceptance.

If authorized later, begin with view-independent albedo and explicit lighting
under a new split and new metrics. Keep geometry frozen at first so appearance
cannot hide geometric error. Texture quality does not waive silhouette,
normal, boundary, or topology gates.

Measurements, sizing, garment patterns, virtual try-on, and product UI remain
outside this roadmap.

## E11 — genus-by-construction source, closed boundary

The constructor guaranteed a serialized Euler-2 surface before it saw private
geometry. Both local and Modal topology gates passed. A later public fidelity
gate froze concavity, external-gap, distance, normal, volume, scale, and rigid-
motion checks before execution. Convex controls passed, but the concave pocket
failed feature/volume limits and the hairpin lost all registered gaps while
more than doubling volume. E11 is closed; no private carrier was read.

P1 remains a separate renderer-correctness diagnostic. Its one-L40S trace
found bitwise-stable forward stages but a geometry-gradient difference of
about `7.45e-9` on the second backward pass. Exact checkpoint replay passed,
but the fixed bitwise gate failed. P1 is closed, E8 stays closed, and no
renderer rewrite is justified without localization to a specific primitive.

A future carrier proposal must therefore remain nonconvex while guaranteeing
one exact embedded Euler-2 component by construction. It requires a new
experiment ID and must not repair, retune, or weaken E11 after its public
failure.

P3 and P4 subsequently isolated collision certification. P3's conservative
partition left 107,581 dense hairpin near pairs and exceeded its narrow-phase
time gate. P4's Planar-DAT gate stopped earlier because a fixed absolute
Tight-Inclusion tolerance rejected 82 small-scale tangent paths. A next public
dependency must preregister a scale-normalized or exact primitive-path oracle
before it can judge Planar-DAT on the refined hairpin. No E16 is active.

P5 subsequently passed that normalized analytic oracle gate. A new public
mesh-level dependency may now be preregistered against the exact P2 hairpin,
but P5 itself does not authorize E16, private geometry, or optimization.

P6 subsequently showed that both refined-hairpin Planar-DAT mechanism calls
exceed the 30-second budget before the normalized oracle runs. Any next public
dependency must reduce trust-region candidate/safe-distance construction with
a complete complement proof. No E16 is active.

Stage profiling further localized the cost to Planar-DAT's direction-aware
mixed-stencil reduction; candidate construction itself was fast. An isotropic
per-vertex trust filter is a possible public comparison, but must retain an
explicit motion gate because upstream documents damping/deadlock risk.

P7 confirmed that risk: isotropic filtering was fast but kept only 2.36% of
tangential motion and restricted every wrap vertex. Do not lower the motion
gate or rerun with a prewarmed worker. No E16 is active.

P8-P11 exhausted the remaining Planar-DAT implementation route. Exact
small-stencil equivalence did not scale to a usable whole-step reference;
scalar/vector and sampled-consensus proxies failed isolated upstream checks.
Full singleton upstream evaluation was finally fast and deterministic, but its
combined endpoint had 317 exact self-intersections and its full-path judge
timed out. Do not register another arithmetic, batching, tolerance, sampling,
or timeout variant. A future collision lane needs a genuinely global path
construction and independent exact endpoint success before private fidelity.

G16/E16 through E21 subsequently exhausted the registered ambient-map route.
The fine exact scaffold was not constructible within its public cap. A coarse
bi-Lipschitz map was overly restrictive. Exact determinant maps improved
tangential retention from `0.07463` to `0.09314` and finally `0.13660` with an
active-tangent direction, but never met `0.25`. Do not propose another grid,
block count, active ratio, tolerance, certificate floor, or time allowance for
this family. E22 changed representation by optimizing and rendering a fixed-
domain PL zero set rather than another ambient deformation, but closed on its
report and fidelity contract. E23's full-rank coordinates delayed rather than
removed complete-path rejection and also closed. E18/E24 remain blocked; B1
and B2 are closed. The E25/B3 registration below supersedes that terminal
no-active-lane checkpoint without reopening either experiment.

## E25/B3 accepted next sequence

E25 changes the representation rather than retuning E22/E23. The treatment is
a visual-hull initialized `24/48/96` neural SDF with NeuS-style ray weights,
SDF-gradient normals, inverse-transpose normal transport, differentiable
FlexiCubes extraction, Eikonal regularization, and bilateral normal
integrability. It retains mask/boundary/normal evidence only and compares
against the frozen G22 control.

Local derivative, transport, extraction, replay, malformed-input, privacy-read,
and public-fixture gates precede one bounded GPU comparison. Search-stage
surfaces cannot be promoted. Exact one-component Euler-2 topology,
embeddedness, gaps/probes, and replay bind every stage boundary. After
commitment, connectivity freezes for certified refinement. Public promotion
requires `<=5°` directional median-normal error, `>=10%` truth-distance
improvement, nonworse held-out image/gap metrics, and every exact/replay gate.

B3 preserves official SelfRecon source/data/schedule and changes only runtime
isolation: absolute Python 3.11 for the control plane and an absolute isolated
Python 3.8 child, with no legacy global `PATH`. It is a new external baseline,
not a B2 retry, and its RGB use must be explicit in every comparison.

E26 remains conditional on an E25-certified representation and a separately
activated private smoke. D3-Human and REC-MV remain serial external-baseline
candidates after B3 terminates. RGB, tracks, and new motion terms remain later
ablations; E18/E24 and the sealed test remain blocked.

## Accepted reproduction-first roadmap

The project now prioritizes the original SelfRecon-style unified outer-surface
target. The corrected order is:

1. reproduce the official/reference public example in an isolated and audited
   runtime, preserving its documented mechanism and schedule;
2. build an isolated adapter from project evidence into that input contract;
3. fit and evaluate one canonical outer SDF on the project video using held-out
   silhouette, boundary, normal, topology, replay, and provenance gates;
4. consider explicit open-garment or material-state reconstruction only after
   the unified surface succeeds.

Earlier SelfRecon operational attempts remain closed under their IDs. A new
reference reproduction must use a newly registered, materially different
runtime artifact and cannot be disguised as an old retry. No pretrained output,
short schedule, proxy input, held-out fitting, or file-existence success is
allowed.

R01 is registered and its zero-GPU local audit passed the pinned source,
research-only/patent notice, official archive bindings, licensed SMPL binding,
and exact 689-frame public RGB/mask/normal alignment. It stopped only because a
new content-addressed prebuilt runtime manifest is absent. The runtime recipe is
frozen, but no artifact was built or pushed. Registry selection and one bounded
external build require an explicit owner decision before runtime import or
device qualification.

Garment necklines, cuffs or armholes, hems, gaps, and folds are regional
diagnostics for the primary track. Four physical loops, material coordinates,
rest metric, contact, and strain are not primary promotion requirements. A
unified shell must not be claimed as a separated garment.

The capture ladder begins with an optional bare-torso regional engineering case,
then a close-fitting clothed case, then loose clothing. Each person/video is
isolated, and a regional or bare-torso result cannot qualify a full clothed-human
endpoint. The deferred MANTLE route may resume only after outer-surface
promotion and a separately accepted contract.
